Information We Collect

When you use Ricoz Webinars, we may collect information that you provide directly to us when creating an account, registering for webinars, or interacting with the platform.

Account Information

This may include your name, email address, and account credentials required to provide authenticated access to the platform.

Webinar Information

We may store information related to webinar registrations, including the webinars you register for and information necessary to provide access to those webinars.

02

How We Use Information

Information collected through Ricoz Webinars may be used to operate and improve the platform and provide the services requested by users.

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Process webinar registrations.
  • Provide access to webinars and related resources.
  • Maintain and improve platform functionality.
  • Protect the security and integrity of the service.
  • Respond to support or administrative requests.
03

Account Information

If you create an account, certain information is required to provide authenticated access to Ricoz Webinars.

Passwords are stored using password-hashing technology rather than being stored as plain-text passwords.

You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential and for notifying the appropriate Ricoz administrator if you believe your account has been compromised.

04

Webinar Information

When you register for a webinar, the platform may store the registration information associated with your account.

This information helps us provide features such as registered-webinar lists, webinar access, and participation management.

05

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by the platform.

These measures may include authentication controls, password hashing, access controls, secure configuration, and protection of sensitive application credentials.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users should also take appropriate steps to protect their own accounts and credentials.

06

Information Sharing

We do not use account information for purposes unrelated to operating and providing the Ricoz Webinars service unless otherwise communicated or required by applicable law.

Information may be disclosed when necessary to operate the service, protect the platform and its users, comply with legal obligations, or respond to lawful requests.

Third-party services may be involved in hosting, infrastructure, database, authentication, analytics, communication, or other technical operations. Where applicable, those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

07

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the platform, maintain account and webinar functionality, satisfy operational requirements, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type and purpose of the information.

08

Your Choices

Depending on the available platform functionality and applicable requirements, you may have options regarding your account information and webinar registrations.

If you have a question about your personal information or need assistance with your account, you can contact the Ricoz team through the appropriate support or administrative channel.

09

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the platform, services, security practices, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page together with the revised "Last updated" date.
